Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Sentryl — AI Email Threat & Forensic Shield (“Sentryl”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) accesses, inspects, processes, and protects information when you use our browser extension and associated threat-intelligence services.

🛡️ Our Fundamental Privacy Commitment

Sentryl is built strictly for in-browser cybersecurity triage. We never sell personal data, we do not inject advertisements, we do not monitor your unrelated web browsing, and we never access your email credentials or contacts.

1. Introduction & Scope

Sentryl is a Manifest V3 browser extension and security intelligence platform designed for Microsoft Edge and Chromium-based browsers. Its single purpose is to perform real-time email forensic analysis — surfacing lookalike domains, credential harvesting links, homoglyph attacks, and SMTP routing anomalies directly inside your webmail interface.

2. What Information Sentryl Accesses

When you actively open an email in supported webmail services (e.g., Google Gmail at mail.google.com), Sentryl parses the Document Object Model (DOM) of the currently opened email message only to extract cybersecurity indicators:

  • Sender Information: The sender's email address and displayed name (used to evaluate lookalike domain spoofing and homoglyph attacks).
  • Subject Line & Text Snippets: Natural language indicators analyzed for urgency pressure, wire transfer fraud (BEC), or credential harvesting cues.
  • Hyperlinks (URLs): Web addresses rendered in the email body (inspected for IP-based hosts, punycode/homoglyph trickery, or high-risk TLDs).
  • Email Headers (when available): Authentication status strings (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and SMTP Received: relay IP hops.
✕ What Sentryl Never Accesses
  • Your email passwords, account credentials, or multi-factor authentication tokens.
  • Your address book, contacts list, calendar, or sent message history.
  • Unopened emails or emails outside the currently active reading pane.
  • Your general browsing history, search queries, cookies, or activity on other tabs.
  • Email file attachments (attachments are ignored and not processed).

4. How Information Is Processed (Local & Hybrid AI Engine)

Sentryl operates using a resilient dual-mode architecture:

  • Client-Side Heuristic Engine: By default, domain similarity algorithms (Levenshtein distance, homoglyph replacement mapping), URL structure checks, and heuristic regex scoring execute 100% locally in your browser runtime without transmitting data.
  • Optional Server-Side Threat API: If enabled by your organization or configured in config.js, the extension transmits email indicators (sender, subject, URLs, headers) to the designated Sentryl backend API endpoint via secure TLS/HTTPS for deep threat verification. If the backend is unavailable or unconfigured, the extension automatically falls back to client-only analysis.

5. Local Storage & Data Retention

Sentryl uses the browser’s sandboxed chrome.storage.local API to temporarily cache the analysis verdict (e.g. risk score, detected indicator tags, and relay hops) for the single last-scanned email.

  • This data resides solely on your physical device and is never synced across devices via cloud accounts.
  • No permanent database of your personal emails is created or retained on Sentryl servers.
  • Backend API processing (when engaged) operates in-memory; forensic report IDs expire from temporary memory caches.

6. Third-Party Services & Disclosures

Sentryl minimizes external network connections:

  • OpenStreetMap (Leaflet Map Tiles): When you open the Extended Forensic Dossier (report.html), map imagery tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap servers (openstreetmap.org) to visualize IP coordinates. No email text, URLs, or user identifiers are sent to OpenStreetMap.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: Sentryl contains no Google Analytics, no telemetry trackers, no advertising pixels, and no user fingerprinting libraries.

7. Data Sharing & Sale

We state unequivocally:

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or email indicators to any third parties or data brokers.
  • We do not share your email data with advertisers or marketing networks.
  • Indicator data is used strictly and exclusively to evaluate email security risk and render threat intelligence to you.

8. Security & Data Protection Measures

We implement industry-standard cybersecurity best practices:

  • Transport Security: All API communication is strictly encrypted over HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • DOM Sanitization: All user-facing UI elements utilize strict DOM text node bindings to prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
  • Manifest V3 Compliance: No remotely hosted JavaScript code is executed; all extension scripts are self-contained and statically verified during store packaging.
  • Input Boundary Limits: Maximum payload length constraints prevent memory exhaustion or denial-of-service attempts.

9. User Controls, Deletion & Opt-Out

You maintain full control over Sentryl at all times:

  • Clear Local Cache: You can clear all cached scan data at any time by right-clicking the Sentryl extension icon → Manage extension → Clear data, or by clearing your browser cache.
  • Extension Removal: Uninstalling the extension immediately purges all locally stored data from chrome.storage.local.
  • Disable In-Page Overlay: You can dismiss or minimize the inline Gmail warning banner at any time using the close (✕) control.

10. Children's Privacy

Sentryl is an enterprise and consumer security tool not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

11. International Transfers

If you utilize an organization-hosted Sentryl backend API, data transmitted for analysis is processed on the servers designated by your deployment configuration in accordance with applicable regional data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA).
